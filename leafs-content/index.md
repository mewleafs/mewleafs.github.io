# Leafs Content

A desktop app for Windows that takes a YouTube video from idea to export: a board of ideas,
structure and script writing, recording with a teleprompter, a timeline editor, thumbnails and
other images, and the description and chapters for YouTube. The interface works in English and
Spanish, and each video can be in either language.

AI helps at every step through the official Claude Code or Codex command-line tools, signed in with
your own subscription. The AI only proposes changes: you accept or reject each one. It never
creates images, video or voices.

Everything is kept in a workspace folder on your PC. There's no Leafs account and no Leafs server.

## Google and YouTube

Connecting YouTube is optional. If you connect it, Leafs Content asks Google for read-only access
(`youtube.readonly` and `yt-analytics.readonly`) to show how your published videos perform (views,
average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, comments and subscribers gained) and, if
you allow it, to let the AI compare new ideas with your past videos. It never uploads, edits or
deletes anything on YouTube. Details are in the [privacy policy](privacy/).
