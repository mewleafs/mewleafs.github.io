# Leafs Content

A desktop app for Windows that takes a YouTube video from idea to export: a board of ideas,
structure and script writing, recording with a teleprompter, B-roll recorded in OBS Studio while
the script's voice plays, a timeline editor, thumbnails, other
images and animations (GIFs, emotes, stickers and short videos for social media) that can also go
into the edit, and the title, tags, chapters and hashtags for YouTube, each ready to copy. It also
turns stream recordings into Shorts: you import a recording of a stream, the AI suggests moments
from it, and only the ones you keep become Shorts. If you stream with Leafs Live, the moments its
chat got excited about, your markers and the viewers' clips from that stream help find them. The interface works in English and Spanish, and
each video can be in either language.

AI helps at every step through the official Claude Code or Codex command-line tools, signed in with
your own subscription. The AI only proposes changes: you accept or reject each one. It never
creates images, video or voices.

Everything is kept in a workspace folder on your PC. There's no Leafs account and no Leafs server.

## Google and YouTube

Connecting YouTube is optional. If you connect it, Leafs Content asks Google for read-only access
(`youtube.readonly` and `yt-analytics.readonly`) to show how your published videos perform (views,
average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, comments and subscribers gained), to show
which parts of a stream's video viewers rewatched or skipped (audience retention) and, if you allow
it, to let the AI compare new ideas with your past videos and look for Shorts where viewers
rewatched. It never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube, and never downloads videos from
YouTube or Twitch. Details are in the [privacy policy](privacy/).
