# GW2 Armoury

An unofficial, fan-made Twitch Extension that shows a streamer's current Guild Wars 2 build (gear, traits, skills, pets
and legends) in a panel, on the video and in the Twitch mobile app, in English, German, Spanish,
French and Chinese.
